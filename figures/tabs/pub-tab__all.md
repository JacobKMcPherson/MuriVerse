**Table 6. Model predictions versus observed intravenous polymyxin half-lives by species and drug. Prediction, median is the median of the four prediction rows for that drug and species (two congeners under two equivalent N-terminal encodings, which give identical model inputs). Fold error is the median of the per-row fold errors, each taken as the larger of prediction/observed and observed/prediction. Prediction / observed is the median prediction divided by the observed value. The last two columns are not reciprocals of one another: fold error is aggregated per row and discards the direction of each error, whereas prediction / observed is a ratio of medians and retains it. In every cell the per-congener predictions lie on one side of the intravenous reference, so the ratio of medians and the median fold error agree in direction.**
