**Supplementary Figure 2. Performance and label reproducibility.** (a) IV-only versus pooled-route Spearman ρ for both-species, mouse and rat strata; circles show the pooled-route model and diamonds the IV-only model. (b) Per-species cross-validated performance: median absolute fold error (left; 3.96 mouse, 2.18 rat, 2.37 both species) and Spearman ρ (right; 0.562, 0.600, 0.574). Fold errors are the median for each of three seeds, averaged over the seeds; ρ values are the headline three-seed means; dashed lines mark a fold error of 1 and ρ of 0.5. (c) Out-of-fold predicted versus observed half-life for the 213-record intravenous-labelled set under cluster- and source-grouped cross-validation. Points are seed 0; ρ = 0.574, r = 0.568 and RMSE = 0.675 log₁₀ h are the headline metrics, the means of three seeds. (d) Between-source label agreement: half-life from source A versus source B for 33 cross-source pairs in the intravenous cohort (r = 0.75, ρ = 0.61; median absolute difference 0.35 log₁₀ units, approximately 2.2-fold). The pairs come from nine sequence–species combinations, and one peptide reported by seven sources supplies 21 of them (triangles), so they are not independent. (e, f) Sensitivity of the original/pre-audit benchmark to label certainty. The ensemble was refitted on each restricted subset of the 213 intravenous-labelled records under the same grouped five-fold protocol (three seeds; Supplementary Table 5), alongside a species-median baseline refitted in each fold. (e) Spearman ρ with 95% group-bootstrap intervals; the sand-coloured bars in the last two rows give the 5th–95th percentile of 30 random subsets of the same size drawn from the cohort (tick, mean), as a rough reference only, since random subsets do not reproduce the source composition of the verified subsets. (f) Mean absolute error (MAE) with 95% intervals for the same subsets. Row labels give each subset and its sample size: All records (the original/pre-audit benchmark), Exact labels, No contradiction (not contradicted by the source), + 3 corrected (with the three corrected values restored), Full text or abstract, Full text only, and Full text, no top patent (verified in full primary-source text, largest patent excluded). (g, h) The ensemble compared with its members and a constant baseline on identical folds (213 records, 55 connected groups; Supplementary Table 3). (g) MAE with 95% group-bootstrap intervals for the equal-weight ensemble, ridge regression, the RBF support-vector regressor, XGBoost and a species median computed within each training fold. (h) Paired ensemble-minus-comparator difference in MAE with 95% intervals; negative values favour the ensemble. Half-life axes are shown in clock time.
