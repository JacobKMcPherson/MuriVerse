**Figure 3. Route dependence and prediction of rodent peptide half-life in MuriVerse.** (a, b) Cross-species and cross-route transfer across the 320-record analysis set. Separately trained models are evaluated on identical held-out species–route subsets, with homology clusters and sources excluded from training. (a) Ranking performance and signed prediction bias; points and bars indicate means ±1 SD across five fold-assignment seeds. (b) Cross-route biases of 0.45–0.65 log₁₀ h contrast with cross-species biases within ±0.21 log₁₀ h, showing a route-associated prediction bias that motivates the route-restricted, species-pooled training design. (c–g) Performance of an ensemble of ridge regression, support-vector regression and XGBoost using molecular annotations, study context, terminal classes and physicochemical descriptors for 213 intravenous-labelled records (179 rat, 34 mouse). Evaluation uses five-fold cross-validation holding out both homology clusters and sources across three seeds. (c) Observed versus predicted half-life by species, with identity and fitted lines. (d) Cumulative absolute fold-error distributions overall and by species; 57% of predictions fall within threefold of observations. (e) Receiver operating characteristic curve for half-life ≥6 h (17 positive records); the curve is seed 0. The AUC of 0.818 is the three-seed mean, with the 95% group-bootstrap interval 0.620–0.877 (as in Table 1); the three seed-specific AUCs range from 0.77 to 0.85, which is a range and not an interval. (f) Median absolute fold error across study and molecular strata (record count in parentheses); bars indicate interquartile ranges. (g) Feature-set performance under alternative grouping schemes; bars indicate 95% group-bootstrap intervals for joint cluster–source grouping. Rows are sorted by Spearman ρ. The row marked by a diamond (bold, grey band) is the nested feature-configuration selection estimate for the shipped feature set, with the configuration chosen inside each training fold (it nests only that choice, and residual optimism may remain): Spearman ρ = 0.460 (95% CI, 0.267–0.548), against 0.574 (95% CI, 0.322–0.655) for the shipped set (bold green label), chosen on the same folds that score it; RMSE of the latter is 0.675 log₁₀ h. Row labels abbreviate the feature blocks: seq (ESM-2 sequence embedding), SMILES (PeptideCLM SMILES embedding), mol (molecule annotation), ctx (study context), term (terminal classes) and phys (physicochemical descriptors); all six blocks combines every block. (h) Paired intravenous and subcutaneous half-lives for 32 sequence–species combinations; subcutaneous half-life exceeds intravenous half-life in 24 pairs (paired Wilcoxon p = 0.001), consistent with absorption-limited terminal kinetics. (i) Half-life distributions in the saved cluster-preserving training and validation split, which defines no cross-validation fold and no cross-validated score and supplies only the baseline constants of the polymyxin application. Panels c, e and f draw seed 0, one out-of-fold pass; the correlations in c, the AUC in e and the accuracy summaries in d (the cumulative curves, the percentages, median fold errors and signed errors) are means over the three seeds. Half-life axes are shown in clock time. Moderate ranking performance, substantial absolute error and between-source variability limit interpretation as calibrated half-life estimates.
