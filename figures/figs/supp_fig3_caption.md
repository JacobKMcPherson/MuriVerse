**Supplementary Figure 3. The polymyxin comparison against observations from every administration route.** This is the secondary, route-pooled counterpart to Figure 4. The model, the predictions and the training-label distribution are identical to Figure 4; only the comparator changes, from intravenous observations alone to observations pooled across intravenous, subcutaneous, intraperitoneal and intramuscular administration. Because the shipped model was trained on intravenous records only, these comparisons are not route-matched and are reported as descriptive context rather than as an assessment of the model. The predicted-versus-observed panel in Figure 4c uses intravenous-only references and is not repeated here. (a) Predicted congener half-lives relative to the intravenous training-label distribution, with the four animal-weighted route-pooled observed drug–species values shown as dotted lines. (b) Predicted half-lives across congeners and species compared with the route-pooled animal-weighted observations and the constant baselines derived from the post-audit intravenous training split. The pooled reference values are 1.02 h for colistin in mice (4 studies), 1.70 h for colistin in rat (7 studies), 1.13 h for polymyxin B in mouse (5 studies) and 1.90 h for polymyxin B in rat (3 studies). (c) Every contributing study drawn separately, by drug and species, with the route and dose of each arm, the half-life type and analysis method, and the animals, matrix and sampling window. Marker fill distinguishes values reported by the study from values derived here; marker color distinguishes the Tam lab and the Monash group from other sources. Grey bands show the pooled observation ±1 SD and colored lines the model prediction for that drug and species. Half-life axes are shown in clock time. The spread among study estimates within a drug–species cell exceeds the distance between the model and the pooled reference in three of the four cells; for rat polymyxin B the three studies agree more closely (1.45–2.07 h) and the model is 3.1-fold below them.
